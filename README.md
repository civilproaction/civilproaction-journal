# CivilProAction-journal

CivilProAction-journal is an open-access scholarly journal dedicated to advancing knowledge, innovation, and practice in civil society, non-governmental organizations (NGOs), community development, humanitarian action, governance, social justice, public policy, and sustainable development.

## Aim

CivilProAction-journal provides an international platform for researchers, practitioners, policymakers, and development professionals to publish high-quality research and evidence-based practice related to civil society and development.

## Scope

- Civil Society Organizations (CSOs)
- Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs)
- Community Development
- Humanitarian Action
- Social Development
- Governance and Public Policy
- Gender and Social Inclusion
- Climate Change and Resilience
- Disaster Risk Reduction
- Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
- Monitoring and Evaluation
- Capacity Development
- Human Rights
- Peacebuilding
- Social Innovation
