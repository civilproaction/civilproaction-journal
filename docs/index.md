# CivilProAction Journal

**Advancing Knowledge for Civil Society and Sustainable Development**

Welcome to **CivilProAction Journal**, an open-access scholarly journal dedicated to publishing high-quality research and practice in civil society, non-governmental organizations, humanitarian action, governance, public policy, and sustainable development.

## Our Mission

To promote evidence-based research, innovation, and practical knowledge that strengthens civil society organizations and contributes to sustainable and inclusive development.

## Journal Scope

- Civil Society Organizations (CSOs)
- Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs)
- Community Development
- Humanitarian Action
- Governance and Public Policy
- Social Development
- Gender Equality and Inclusion
- Climate Change
- Disaster Risk Reduction
- Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
